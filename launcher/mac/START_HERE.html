Sonic Riders on iPhone and iPad

Start here. The first start of the launcher needs four clicks in macOS, once. After that the launcher guides you through everything else (Xcode, building, Sideloadly, Developer Mode, renewing).

Before you start

0

Unzip and keep the folder

Double-click the zip you downloaded. Keep the folder it makes (for example move it into your home folder): the launcher builds the app inside it and needs it again for updates. Its path must not have spaces; if it does, the launcher offers to move it for you.

The launcher is free and unofficial, so it isn't signed by Apple (that costs $99 a year). That is why macOS asks you to allow it once, as shown below.

The first start (once)

1

Double-click "Sonic Riders Launcher"

It's in the folder you unzipped (the Super Sonic icon).

2

A warning appears: click Done

macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done. Do not click "Move to Trash" (that deletes the launcher; if it happened, unzip again).

System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway

Open System Settings (Apple menu > System Settings), click Privacy & Security in the sidebar and scroll down to Security. Next to the launcher's name there is now an Open Anyway button (it only appears after step 1). Click it and confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.

Then, in the launcher

✓

Get ready: checks Xcode (free, App Store), its iOS platform and Metal Toolchain, and Homebrew's two build tools. Each missing piece has a button that installs or opens it.

Build: pick your own Sonic Riders (USA) disc image and, if you like, any picture as the app icon. The first build takes 15-30 minutes.

Install: step by step with Sideloadly (free): connect the device, sign in with your Apple ID, Developer Mode, trust your Apple ID on the device, copy the disc image.

Renew: with a free Apple ID the app works for 7 days; renewing takes a minute and keeps your saves.

The Terminal way still works: "Install Sonic Riders (iOS).command" in the same folder (it needs the same Privacy & Security step). README-FIRST.txt has all the details. Never share your disc image or the .ipa: they contain the game.
