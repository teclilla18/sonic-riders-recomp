02 / DOWNLOAD

Choose your machine

Each button downloads one ZIP file straight from GitHub. You don't need a GitHub account.

!

New to this? It takes three things:

  1. Your own copy of Sonic Riders (USA) for GameCube, saved as a disc image (.iso or Dolphin .rvz). We don't provide the game.
  2. A computer to build on: Windows 10/11 for most consoles, the device itself on Linux, a Mac for iPhone / iPad.
  3. About 10–30 minutes the first time. Updates take about a minute.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to the start line

01

Download the setup

One ZIP for your device. It contains the installer and no game files.

02

Pick your disc image

The installer checks it, then translates the game's code into a native app on your computer.

03

Race

On Switch it copies everything to your SD card. On PC press PLAY. Later updates: one button.

Why build it yourself? The finished game contains SEGA's code, so it can never be shared as a download. It is always made on your computer, from a disc you own.

06 / ROAD TO v1.0

Lap progress

v1.0 means the whole game plays like the GameCube original on every supported platform, Switch races hold their frame rate, and you can race friends online. These numbers come straight from the repository.

Overall

77%

Judged by hand at each release, weighted by area (whole game 25 %, the rest 15 % each). Last updated Nov 18, 2026 for v0.8.7.

  1. Whole game playableweight 25 %90 %

    Grand Prix freeze after character select (#18), rare "Now Loading" hang (#6)

  2. Graphics match the GameCubeweight 15 %80 %

    Lens flare (#7), story cutscene monitor (#4), stronger speed blur (#3)

  3. Switch performanceweight 15 %70 %

    60 fps at stock clocks (#1), 3-4 player races at 60 with an overclock (#2) (done on main: heavy 1P views hold 60 at 1.5x with picture options, overclocked)

  4. Platformsweight 15 %85 %

    Android #32 resolved: Vulkan 1.1 race/clean quit confirmed on Mali; MoltenVK pending. Linux / Steam Deck out of test, Switch Lite / original / docked reports (#9)

  5. Online playweight 15 %55 %

    PC-PC, PC-Switch and Switch-Switch races stay in sync (tested over home networks); each player keeps their own screen shape; still to do: internet play (lobby server with room codes), a room-wide 30 fps switch for slow consoles, phones, 3-4 players, ping and delay settings

  6. Features and polishweight 15 %72 %

    Responsive installers, DPI/completion and output-folder action tested. Remaining: custom music, right-stick camera, code mods/new characters and background texture-pack loading on Switch.
