Find a lobby

      Race lobby

        In-game invite

        Open your in-game internet lobby first. Share its code with a race partner you trust. The code can contain your public IP address.

        Race Room names are generated from a random browser ID. Public quick chat and lobby details expire after 30 minutes. Lobby chat and game invites are visible to the host and their race partner.
