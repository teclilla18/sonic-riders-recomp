Updating Sonic Riders

Pick your device, then your version, and follow only that part. Tap a step to tick it off.

Colour key
  • Pink = a button to press
  • Teal = a file or program
  • Purple = SD card / hekate
  • Gold = what you'll see on screen
🔵 I'm on v0.8.2 or olderOne-time setup, then you're done 🟢 I'm on v0.8.3 or newerOne-press update

Coming from v0.8.2 or older one time only

  1. Download SonicRiders-Switch-Setup-v0.8.3.zip from the latest release.
  2. Extract it over your old install folder. When asked, choose Replace. A new folder works too.
  3. Connect your SD card to the PC. How: see Connecting the SD card below.
  4. Open Install Sonic Riders (Switch).exe. Pick the same disc image → check your SD card is selected → press BUILD AND INSTALL.
  5. When it says Done, eject the SD card and play. This one time it builds the whole game (a few minutes, up to 30 on a slow PC). Later updates take seconds.

From v0.8.3 on one press

  1. Open Install Sonic Riders (Switch).exe. When a new version is out, it shows Update available in gold.
  2. Connect your SD card. How: see Connecting the SD card below.
  3. Press See what's new, then UPDATE NOW. That's all. It downloads, restarts, rebuilds and copies to your SD card by itself, usually in under a minute.
  4. Eject the SD card and play.
💡
SD card not connected yet? No problem. The updater waits for it and carries on as soon as it shows up.

Connecting the SD card no need to take it out

  1. Restart the Switch into hekate. The way you normally start your custom firmware.
  2. Go to Tools → USB Tools → SD Card.
  3. Plug the Switch into the PC with a USB-C cable. The SD card shows up as a drive.
  4. When the update says Done, right-click the drive in Windows and choose Eject. Wait until Windows says it's safe to remove.
  5. In hekate press Close, then Home → Launch → your Atmosphère entry.
⚠️
Windows offers to scan and fix the drive? Choose Continue without scanning.
💳
Have a card reader? That works too. Just take the SD card out and put it in the PC.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves, settings, disc image and HOME-screen icon stay as they are. You don't need to reinstall or copy anything else.

🔍 Check it worked

In the game, hold − to open the settings menu.

Look at the version number in the About tab.

🔵 I'm on v0.8.2 or olderA few presses, one time 🟢 I'm on v0.8.3 or newerOne-press update

Coming from v0.8.2 or older one time only

  1. Open the Sonic Riders launcher. It shows Update available in gold.
  2. Press See what's new, then DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL.
  3. When it says Updated, press RESTART.
  4. On the Play page, press Rebuild now. This one time it builds the whole game (a few minutes). Later updates take seconds.
  5. When it's done, press PLAY.
📦
Prefer the zip? Download SonicRiders-PC-Setup-v0.8.3.zip, extract it over your old Sonic Riders folder (Replace), and run Install Sonic Riders (PC).bat with the same disc image.

From v0.8.3 on one press

  1. Open the Sonic Riders launcher. When a new version is out, it shows Update available in gold.
  2. Press See what's new, then UPDATE NOW. It downloads, restarts and rebuilds by itself, usually in under a minute.
  3. Press PLAY.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves, settings, mods and disc image stay in the same folder.

🔍 Check it worked

In the game, hold F1 to open the settings menu.

Look at the version number in the About tab.

🔵 I'm on v0.8.2 or olderOne-time setup, then you're done 🟢 I'm on v0.8.3 or newerOne-press update

Coming from v0.8.2 or older one time only

  1. Download SonicRiders-Android-Setup-v0.8.3.zip from the latest release.
  2. Extract it over your old install folder. When asked, choose Replace.
  3. Connect your phone with USB debugging on. Use the same PC as before: Android only accepts an update signed by that PC.
  4. Open Install Sonic Riders (Android).exe. Pick the same disc image → press BUILD AND INSTALL.
  5. When it says Done, open the app on your phone. It installs over the old app and keeps your saves. This one time the build takes a few minutes.

From v0.8.3 on one press

  1. Connect your phone and open Install Sonic Riders (Android).exe. When a new version is out, it shows Update available in gold.
  2. Press See what's new, then UPDATE NOW. It downloads, restarts, rebuilds and installs on the phone by itself.
  3. Open the app on your phone.
⚠️
Never uninstall the app to update. Uninstalling deletes your saves.

🔍 Check it worked

In the game, open the settings menu.

Look at the version number in the About tab.

Every version no one-press button on Linux yet

  1. Download SonicRiders-Linux-Setup-v0.8.3.zip from the latest release.
  2. Extract it over your old install folder. When asked, choose Replace. On the Steam Deck, do this in Desktop Mode.
  3. Run Install Sonic Riders (Linux).sh with the same disc image. The first time on v0.8.3 builds the whole game once. After that, only the parts that changed are rebuilt.
  4. When it says Done, play as usual.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves stay in the same folder, and your Steam shortcut keeps working.
💡
Nothing new for iPhone and iPad in v0.8.3. Keep using v0.8.2: SonicRiders-iOS-Setup-v0.8.2.zip on the v0.8.2 release.

Why is there still a short build? The finished game contains Sonic Riders' own code, so it can never be put in a download. It's always made on your PC from your own disc. The big conversion happens only on the first install; updates only rebuild the parts that changed. Full guide: INSTALL.md.
