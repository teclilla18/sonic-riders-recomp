Updating Sonic Riders

Pick your device, then your version, and follow only that part. Tap a step to tick it off.

🌐 New in v0.10.0

Race your friends online

PC, Switch and Android can race each other, in the same house or across the internet. Two players for now: a 4-player update is coming soon.

Online on: 💻 Windows PC · 🎮 Nintendo Switch (original, Lite, OLED) · 📱 Android (64-bit).
Not yet on Switch 2, Linux / Steam Deck or iPhone / iPad.

  1. Update both devices to v0.10.2 with the steps below. v0.10.0 and v0.10.2 can race each other.
  2. In the game, open the settings menu (F1 on PC), choose Online, then Open Online. On a Switch, start Sonic Riders Online instead.
  3. Same house: one player presses Host a race, the other picks it from the list. Different homes: swap invite codes in a private message.

No one to race yet? The Race Room is our live chat: find a racer, say hi and open a lobby together.

🔵 I'm on v0.8.2 or olderOne-time setup, then you're done 🟢 I'm on v0.8.3 or newerOne-press update

Coming from v0.8.2 or older one time only

  1. Download SonicRiders-Switch-Setup-v0.10.2.zip from the latest release.
  2. Extract it over your old install folder. When asked, choose Replace. A new folder works too.
  3. Connect your SD card to the PC. How: see Connecting the SD card below.
  4. Open Install Sonic Riders (Switch).exe. Pick the same disc image → check your SD card is selected → press BUILD AND INSTALL.
  5. When it says Done, eject the SD card and play. This one time it builds the whole game (a few minutes, up to 30 on a slow PC). Later updates take seconds.

From v0.8.3 on one press

  1. Open Install Sonic Riders (Switch).exe. When a new version is out, it shows Update available in gold.
  2. Connect your SD card. How: see Connecting the SD card below.
  3. Press See what's new, then UPDATE NOW. That's all. It downloads, restarts, rebuilds and copies to your SD card by itself, usually in under a minute.
  4. Eject the SD card and play.
💡
SD card not connected yet? No problem. The updater waits for it and carries on as soon as it shows up.

Connecting the SD card no need to take it out

  1. Restart the Switch into hekate. The way you normally start your custom firmware.
  2. Go to Tools → USB Tools → SD Card.
  3. Plug the Switch into the PC with a USB-C cable. The SD card shows up as a drive.
  4. When the update says Done, press Eject SD card in the installer. Wait for Safe to remove the SD card now. (On v0.8.3 and older: right-click the drive in Windows and choose Eject.)
  5. In hekate press Close, then Home → Launch → your Atmosphère entry.
⚠️
Windows offers to scan and fix the drive? Choose Continue without scanning.
💳
Have a card reader? That works too. Just take the SD card out and put it in the PC.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves, settings, disc image and HOME-screen icon stay as they are. You don't need to reinstall or copy anything else.

Nintendo Switch 2 experimental Alpha 3

ℹ️
Switch 2 alpha updates are manual. They are separate from the original Switch’s UPDATE NOW feature and the original Switch stable release. Do not use an original Switch installer to update this target.
  1. Quit the game normally before updating. Keep your SD card data.Do not delete /switch/sonicriders-switch2/, its saves, settings.ini, env.txt or shader caches.
  2. Open the Switch 2 Alpha 3 release and download SonicRiders-Switch2-Setup-Alpha3.zip.Use the dedicated Switch 2 alpha release for future updates too, not the stable latest-release button.
  3. Extract to a new PC folder and open Install Sonic Riders (Switch2).exe.Pick the same validated USA disc image and your SD card; read the shader compiler license, then press BUILD AND INSTALL.
  4. Let the installer replace the NRO and build information only.Existing saves, settings, caches and environment stay. The first build of a changed version may take a while.
  5. If using FTP, copy the newly built NRO and build-info.json to /switch/sonicriders-switch2/.Do not overwrite existing env.txt/settings.ini or delete saves/caches. Your own disc image stays on the card.
  6. Eject the SD card safely if you used a card reader, then launch and check the version.Compatible native homebrew is still required; the reported tested setup is firmware 23.0.1 with kaiHEN 20261117.1 through Seedsow.
⚠️
This is an early alpha. You may see stuttering or temporary freezes, missing music/announcer audio, temporary distortion and slow startup. Unlimited rendering is an experimental opt-in; normal 60 FPS remains the default. Fresh defaults are 2x, 60 FPS and no time limit; live resolution changes are disabled.

Playing, quitting and reporting a problem

  1. Launch through your compatible native homebrew setup.The tested flow: hold R while starting a native Switch 2 game, then choose Sonic Riders in Homebrew Menu.
  2. To exit normally, hold Minus for about half a second. Choose Quit Game and confirm.You should return to Homebrew Menu. There is no automatic exit timer.
  3. Report a bug with the alpha/build version and what you did.Include firmware, loader version, handheld/docked mode, known clocks, stage, character and gear. Save sonicriders.log, sonicriders.prev.log and lifecycle logs from /switch/sonicriders-switch2/ before relaunching. Review private paths first. Never share your disc image, NRO, saves or keys.

🔵 I'm on v0.8.2 or olderA few presses, one time 🟢 I'm on v0.8.3 or newerOne-press update

Coming from v0.8.2 or older one time only

  1. Open the Sonic Riders launcher. It shows Update available in gold.
  2. Press See what's new, then DOWNLOAD AND INSTALL.
  3. When it says Updated, press RESTART.
  4. On the Play page, press Rebuild now. This one time it builds the whole game (a few minutes). Later updates take seconds.
  5. When it's done, press PLAY.
📦

  1. Open the Sonic Riders launcher. When a new version is out, it shows Update available in gold.
  2. Press See what's new, then UPDATE NOW. It downloads, restarts and rebuilds by itself, usually in under a minute.
  3. Press PLAY.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves, settings, mods and disc image stay in the same folder.

Full no-PC guide

Every version no one-press button on Linux yet

  1. Download SonicRiders-Linux-Setup-v0.9.0.zip from the v0.9.0 release.
  2. Extract it over your old install folder. When asked, choose Replace. On the Steam Deck, do this in Desktop Mode.
  3. Run Install Sonic Riders (Linux).sh with the same disc image. The first time on v0.8.3 builds the whole game once. After that, only the parts that changed are rebuilt.
  4. When it says Done, play as usual.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves stay in the same folder, and your Steam shortcut keeps working.

Every version no one-press button on iOS yet

  1. On your Mac, download SonicRiders-iOS-Setup-v0.9.0.zip from the v0.9.0 release.
  2. Unzip it into a folder whose path has no spaces. Your home folder works, for example ~/SonicRiders.
  3. Open Sonic Riders Launcher from the new folder. If macOS blocks it, follow START HERE (Mac).html in the same folder.
  4. On the Build page, pick the same disc image and press BUILD THE APP. The first time on v0.8.3 it builds the whole game once (a few minutes). After that, updates only rebuild the parts that changed, usually in under a minute. When it says Done, the new SonicRiders.ipa is in Desktop/Sonic Riders iOS.
  5. On the Install page, install it over the old app. With Sideloadly: Open SonicRiders.ipa in Sideloadly, then Start. From the Mac: Install only (renewing).
⚠️
Never delete the app to update. Deleting it removes your saves and the disc image copied to it.
✅
Nothing else to do. Your saves and disc image stay on the device, and installing starts a new 7 days before you need to renew.

🌐 Play online beta

Race one friend on Windows PC, the original Nintendo Switch or Android, including the no-PC app. Update both devices to the same version first.

  1. Open the game's settings, choose Online, then press Open Online. On a Switch, start Sonic Riders Online instead.The game restarts into the lobby.
  2. On the same network, have one player host a race and the other choose it from the list.
  3. From different homes, choose Host an internet race or Join with a code.Share invite codes privately. They contain an internet address.

Full online play guide

Why is there still a short build? The finished game contains Sonic Riders' own code, so it can never be put in a download. The app or installer builds it locally from your own disc. The big conversion happens only on the first install; updates only rebuild the parts that changed. Installing for the first time? Use the install guide. Full guide: INSTALL.md.
