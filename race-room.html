        Race Room rules

        The Race Room is for finding races and talking about Riders Rebuilt. Keep it fun for everyone, kids included.

        1. Be kind. No insults, bullying, hate, threats or harassment. Trash talk stays friendly.
        2. Keep it clean. No sexual, gory or shocking messages or GIFs. GIFs are filtered to family-friendly ones.
        3. No links. Links can’t be sent, and writing them around the filter (like “site dot com”) counts as spam.
        4. No spam. Don’t flood the chat, repeat the same message or advertise.
        5. Stay safe. Never share your real name, address, phone number, passwords or accounts. Only give an in-game invite code to someone you trust: it can contain your IP address.
        6. No pretending. Don’t pretend to be the Game Dev or another player. The real Game Dev always shows in gold.
        7. No cheating talk or piracy. Don’t ask for or share game files (ISOs). Everyone uses their own disc.
        8. Pick a fair name. Names follow the same rules. Rude names get muted.

        What happens if someone breaks them

        The Game Dev can delete messages and mute riders. Muted riders can’t chat, rename or make lobbies. Messages disappear on their own after 30 minutes.

        Something wrong?

        Report a problem on the Community page.

        Race Room names are generated from a random browser ID. You can pick your own name. Public chat and lobby details expire after 30 minutes. GIFs are shown from GIPHY. Lobby chat and game invites are visible to the host and their race partner.
